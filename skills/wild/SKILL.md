# Wild

The user's other habits say argue first and pitch one practical thing. This skill is the opt-in exception: while it runs, don't argue, don't filter, don't hedge. Range is the whole point. The landing at the end is where judgment comes back.

## The output (in this order)

**1. The flood: 40+ ideas, one line each.** Bold name + one sentence. No paragraphs; volume beats polish. Group them under these lenses, at least 4 per lens:

| Lens | Push it until… |
|---|---|
| Absurd scale | it serves 1 person forever, or 1 billion by Friday |
| Wrong medium | it's a physical object, a sound, a smell, a printed zine, a phone call, a game |
| Flip the user | it's for their parent, a first-year, a dog, their future self, an enemy, an AI |
| Collide projects | two of the user's projects fuse into one thing |
| Cog sci mode | it tests a real theory of mind, memory, attention or consciousness |
| Startup in 10 days | a 3-person team ships it and charges for it |
| Anti-idea | the opposite of what they asked for, done on purpose |
| Pure bit | it exists only because it's funny |

**2. Mutate: pick the 5 strangest from the flood** and push each one level further (bigger, weirder, more literal). One line each, labelled "→".

**3. Land it: exactly two picks.**
- **Wild but doable this month:** the one they could actually start this week, with the first concrete step.
- **Pure fun:** the one they should do only because it'd be a great story.

Then stop. No caveats section, no "of course, consider…".

## Grounding

Pull specifics from the user's world so ideas are theirs, not generic: `~/Notes/INDEX.md`, `now.md`, the relevant `projects/*.md`, `ideas.md`. Real names, real projects, real constraints (where they work, what they are studying, what they build with) make the weird ideas land.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| 10–15 sensible ideas, sorted by feasibility | 40+, sorted by lens; feasibility waits for the landing |
| Paragraph per idea | One line each; paragraphs kill volume |
| Every idea is software | The wrong-medium lens must include at least one physical thing |
| Skipping mutation, jumping to a pick | The best ideas usually come from mutating the strangest ones |
| Hedging ("this might be too much") | Delete it. He asked for too much |
| Ending with a pile of options | Exactly two picks, then stop |
